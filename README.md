🔎 AstroSage Analysis
A data-driven project focused on improving customer satisfaction (CSAT), revenue performance, and infrastructure planning through Excel-based analysis.

📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes 28,000+ customer service records from AstroSage, an astrology consultation platform that handles more than 8,000 calls every day.
Although the platform had a large customer base and high call volumes, satisfaction among returning customers was declining. The goal of this analysis was to identify the major reasons behind this decline and determine how a ₹1 Crore investment could be used effectively to improve customer experience, service quality, and revenue.
⚙️ Tools & Techniques
Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, validation, analysis, and What-If Analysis.
Pivot Tables: Used to summarize and compare revenue and operational performance.
Statistical Analysis: Pearson correlation using =CORREL to study the relationship between consultation duration and CSAT.
Data Visualization: Interactive Excel dashboards with slicers for business reporting.

🎯 Key Findings
1. Chat Drives More Revenue
Revenue analysis showed that Chat was the stronger revenue-generating channel compared with Voice Calls.
At the same time, a large number of incomplete chat sessions suggested technical issues that could be resulting in missed revenue opportunities and a poor customer experience.
2. Longer Sessions Improve Satisfaction
The analysis found a positive correlation of 0.48 between consultation duration and customer satisfaction.
This suggests that customers involved in longer and more detailed consultations were more likely to give higher ratings, including 5-star ratings.
3. Returning Customers Show Lower Satisfaction
First-time users had an average rating of around 4.88, whereas frequent returning users had an average rating of approximately 2.48.
This significant difference pointed toward a possible issue with the existing random-routing approach, where loyal customers were not consistently connected with consultants they had previously rated highly.

💰 Recommended ₹1 Crore Investment Plan
Instead of immediately increasing the workforce, the analysis recommended focusing the investment on technology, employee development, and customer retention.
🖥️ 40% — Technology Upgrade: ₹40 Lakhs
Improve the Chat platform with AI-supported triage and predictive text to handle more concurrent sessions, reduce incomplete consultations, and strengthen the platform's highest-revenue channel.
🎓 40% — Training & Performance: ₹40 Lakhs
Revise agent performance measures to focus more on consultation quality and customer engagement rather than simply completing sessions quickly.
Training and incentives would encourage agents to provide deeper, higher-quality consultations based on the observed 0.48 CSAT correlation.
🔄 20% — Loyalty Routing & CRM: ₹20 Lakhs
Introduce a smarter routing system that identifies returning customers and reconnects them with consultants they previously rated positively.
This could improve customer retention and help reduce the satisfaction gap between new and repeat users.
